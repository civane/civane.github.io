Research

Academic Projects

Jan 2025 – Jun 2025
RAW Domain Diffusion Model

Training framework for a RAW-domain Diffusion model (RAW → clear RGB). Proposed LVAE for latent extraction in RAW domain and a novel RAW-domain prompt extraction method. Outperforms SOTA by 3.2 dB PSNR on DRealSR dataset.

Dec 2024 – May 2025
Burstormer Fusion Network

Multi-frame RAW fusion network using deformable convolution for feature alignment. Introduced blur kernel estimation for built-in deblur capability. Improved PSNR by 0.8 dB in low-light conditions.

Aug 2024 – Dec 2024
Hi-Mamba: Hierarchical Mamba for Image Super-Resolution

Redesigned Mamba scanning mechanism for dynamic scenes. Introduced Hierarchical Mamba Block (HMB) with L-SSM and R-SSM for multi-scale spatial perception. Achieves +0.2 dB PSNR over DLGSANet on Urban100 ×3 SR.

Industry Projects

Engineering Work

Jan 2025 – Jun 2025
One-Step Diffusion Super-Resolution Model

Built on Stable Diffusion 1.5 with custom LoRA modules. GAN architecture integrating single-step diffusion + discriminator. Combined SFT and DPO for image quality. Successfully deployed on mobile with >90% generation success rate.

Apr 2024 – Oct 2024
Staggered HDR (MEF + Knowledge Distillation)

Staggered HDR technology using short-exposure frames as alignment reference, reducing motion blur. Integrated VGG for joint deep training with label distillation and negative sample augmentation. Lightweight model for real-time mobile processing.

Dec 2023 – May 2024
Novel Deblurring Algorithm (KD + Deblur)

Deblurring algorithm for fast-moving pets using SAM4IR knowledge distillation on NARFNet. Deployed to cloud cameras enabling real-time high-definition output.

Mar 2023 – Dec 2023
Advanced Macro Deblurring

NRKNet-based deblurring for macro photography shallow focal plane. Hybrid U-Net + LSTM for defocus and motion blur. 8-bit quantization: 25% model size reduction, 50ms faster NPU inference.
